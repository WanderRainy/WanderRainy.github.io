Road Extraction

How can we recover connected road networks from high-resolution satellite imagery when trees and other objects hide parts of the road? My work explores occlusion-aware segmentation and direct extraction of vector road graphs.

IEEE TGRS · 2024

IS-RoadDet

Directly predicts intersections and road segments with a Transformer to construct vector road graphs from high-resolution imagery.

Paper

IEEE TGRS · 2024

Occlusion-Aware Road Extraction Network

Improves road extraction in high-resolution imagery where parts of the road are obscured.

Paper

OurNet

Studies occluded urban roads with a global high-resolution benchmark and guided generation to recover missing road information.

Multimodal Remote Sensing Foundation Models

How can models combine the fine spatial detail of optical imagery with the all-weather sensing capabilities of synthetic aperture radar (SAR)? My work focuses on learning across sensors and enabling fine-grained interpretation of very-high-resolution scenes.

AAAI · 2026

MaRS

A very-high-resolution foundation model that learns shared optical and SAR representations across spatial scales. It is pretrained on MaRS-16M, a dataset of more than 16 million paired samples.

Project Paper Code

All-Weather Damage Understanding

Explores how a multimodal foundation model can support fine-grained damage interpretation when clouds limit optical observations.

Vision–Language Active Perception

Uses a global overview and adaptive local crops to support visual reasoning and localization in very-high-resolution remote sensing imagery.
